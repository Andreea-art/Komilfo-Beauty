01The Studio

Skin, body, nails & lashes — a complete beauty regimen, under one roof in the heart of Dundalk.

Komilfo is a beauty and aesthetic studio at Cois Croinn, Saltown. The practice spans advanced aesthetics — injectable biostimulators, dermal implants and fillers, thread lifting, mesotherapy and lipolytic injections — alongside the rituals of a classic beauty salon.

Every appointment begins with a consultation, so each treatment plan is built around your skin, your goals and your time.

Device-based treatments include LPG massage for face and body, vacuum RF massage, cavitation and RF rejuvenation, diamond and hydro microdermabrasion, microcurrent therapy and HydroLuxx facials.

Eyelash extensions, face and body waxing, and manicures & pedicures complete the menu — all rated five stars by the studio's clients.
